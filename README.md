### 👋 About

I'm a **GTM Engineer at Gamic Media** and a part-time GTM engineer at **SyncGTM**. I design the infrastructure behind AI-driven outbound for M&A advisory firms: signal detection, lead sourcing and verification, enrichment, and campaign systems that run with very little manual work.

Before that, I built backend and ML services (FastAPI, Django) and wrote research on explainable credit-risk models. My computer engineering degree is from Nepal Engineering College (Pokhara University).

**What I'm focused on right now**

- 🤖 Agentic workflows that research, qualify, and route leads end to end
- ⚙️ Automation pipelines connecting data sources, enrichment, and sending infrastructure
- 📡 Signal detection for the M&A, PE, and investment banking niche
- 🎓 Preparing for a Master's in applied AI/ML in the US
